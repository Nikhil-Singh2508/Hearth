# Business rules

Approved product rules are captured in the originating product brief and implementation plan. Invitation acceptance creates a tenant-only account. Tokens are random, stored as hashes, expire after seven days, and may be accepted once or revoked. Tenancy date intervals are half-open: the move-out date is exclusive. New tenancies create one primary financially responsible tenant and an effective-dated INR rent term. Rent, payment, deposit, utility, and expense amounts use Decimal arithmetic. Utility bills are allocated to tenancies whose periods overlap the bill; posting requires allocations to reconcile exactly to the bill total.

- Billing is monthly; due days beyond a month's length resolve to its last day.
- Proration defaults to actual calendar days; a fixed 30-day option is agreement-specific.
- Fees apply to overdue principal only by default, with grace period, partial-payment basis and cap specified by the agreement.
- Late fee rules are effective-dated per tenancy. Owners assess fees manually after the due date. Fixed fees are one-time, daily fees accrue for each chargeable day, and percentage fees are calculated from the configured rent principal basis. Repeated assessment on the same date is idempotent; assessments must be chronological. Each assessment records a snapshot, and no fee is ever charged on another fee. Daily assessment catches up the cumulative amount through the selected assessment date; subsequent payments can reduce the basis when the rule uses remaining principal.
- Overpayments remain positive tenant credits; payment allocation cannot exceed payment availability or charge balance.
- Shared utility allocations must sum exactly to the bill, with any rounding adjustment explicit.
- A tenancy replacement closes the former participation period and never transfers liability automatically.
- Email and WhatsApp preferences are off by default. A tenant can grant or revoke consent from the tenant portal; WhatsApp opt-in requires an international-format phone number. Grant/revoke events are retained with the consent text version, and the phone is removed on opt-out. Delivery remains unavailable until an actual provider integration is configured; recording consent alone never sends a message.
- Manual payments are recorded as unapplied tenant credits; an owner allocates credit to charges explicitly. Reusing an idempotency key with different payment details is rejected.
- A tenancy's agreed security deposit is nonnegative INR. Receipts are accepted only while the tenancy is active and cannot exceed the agreed deposit. Refunds and deductions are only recorded after close-out, require a reason and cannot exceed the remaining liability. Entries are immutable and idempotent; the system records the accounting transaction but does not move money through a bank.
- Monthly rent posting is unique per tenancy and billing month. Posting currently requires one effective rent term across all billable days; a mid-month term change requires manual resolution until segmented proration is added.
- Utility bills remain pending until the owner posts a complete allocation. Each allocation must belong to the bill's property (and apartment, for apartment-specific bills) and overlap the billing period. Bills and allocations are immutable after posting; corrections require a future reversal workflow. Property expenses are owner records and are never automatically billed to tenants.
- Utility meters belong to one property and optionally one apartment. Readings are append-only, unique per meter/date, cannot go backward, and must be entered in date order. The first reading is a baseline; subsequent readings calculate consumption. The applicable effective-dated apartment rate wins over the property-wide rate, using the reading date. The calculated amount is consumption times the unit rate plus any fixed charge, rounded half-up to INR paise. A positive result creates a pending utility bill; the owner still reconciles allocations before tenant charges are posted. Rates cannot be backdated and are immutable after their effective date starts.
- Move-in and move-out inspections are attached to a tenancy and contain checklist items with a condition and optional notes/evidence. An inspection can be completed only after each checklist item has a recorded condition; completed inspections and their evidence cannot be changed. Tenants can read inspection records only when the owner marked them visible and only for their own tenancy participation. Inspection files are private PDF, JPEG or PNG uploads, limited to 10 MB and signature checked.
- Owner dashboard figures are calculated from persisted records and use half-open calendar periods. Occupancy includes active, currently in-period tenancies; payments received excludes reversed payments; rent charges are grouped by charge period; overdue balances use recorded payment and credit allocations. The rent/payment chart is a six-month view. Missing records are displayed as zero rather than synthetic estimates.
- CSV exports are owner-only, period bounded and capped at 10,000 rows. Formula-leading text is prefixed so spreadsheets treat it as text; exports omit authentication data, tenant email addresses and private files.
